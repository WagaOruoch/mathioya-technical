About Our Institution

A Beacon of Technical Excellence in Murang'a

Mathioya Technical & Vocational College is a National Public Tertiary Institution under the Ministry of Education, established under the Education Act and operating under the TVET Act 2013.

Located in scenic Kamacharia, Mathioya Sub-County, the institution was founded to democratize access to industry-relevant technical competencies. We train young men and women to become inventive craftsmen, technicians, and entrepreneurs capable of driving Kenya's industrial development agenda.

Vision: To be a world-class technical college for sustainable development.

Mission: To produce globally competitive graduates through competency-based education and training (CBET).

Institutional Journey

Our Milestones of Growth

From community vision and ground-breaking to full TVETA accreditation and our historic first graduation ceremony.

2014
Community Land Donation & Groundbreaking: The visionary Mathioya community generously donated the serene Kamacharia land parcel. On 2nd October 2014, the official foundation stone was solemnly laid by the Government of Kenya to kickstart construction of the tertiary technical college.
2014 – 2017
Infrastructure & Workshop Construction: Full construction of modern lecture theatres, administrative offices, civil laboratories, electrical demonstration rooms, and executive administration offices was completed under Ministry of Education supervision.
2017
Official TVETA Registration (25 April 2017): Mathioya Technical and Vocational College was formally awarded legal accreditation and registered by the Technical and Vocational Education and Training Authority (TVETA) on 25 April 2017.
2018
First Trainee Cohort Admitted: The college proudly opened its doors to its first batch of government and self-sponsored trainees across mechanical, electrical, building, business, and ICT faculties.
5 February 2027
Historic First-ever Graduation Ceremony: Marking our ultimate institutional triumph as we confer diplomas and certificates upon our pioneering cohort of highly competent TVET CDACC graduates.
Admissions 2026/2027

How to Apply for Admission

We admit new students across three major intake windows every year: January, May, and September. Both Government-sponsored (via KUCCPS) and Self-sponsored students are welcomed.

  1. Select Your Desired Programme: Choose from our Diploma (KCSE C-), Certificate (KCSE D), or Artisan/Short Courses.
  2. Download & Complete Application Forms: Fill out the standardized application and medical examination certificates.
  3. Submit & Enrol via KUCCPS or In-Person: Bring documents to our Kamacharia campus, send via email, or select Mathioya TVC on KUCCPS.

HELB & Government Capitation: All registered Mathioya TVC students are eligible for Higher Education Loans Board (HELB) tuition loans and Government training capitation.
